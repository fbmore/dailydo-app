Private by architecture

No account. No analytics. Your data lives on your devices and in your own private iCloud — we couldn't read it if we wanted to.

Your data never meets our servers.

DailyDo has no sign-up, no tracking, and no backend. Everything you capture is stored on-device and synced through your personal Apple iCloud account. Read the privacy policy — it's short, because there's not much to disclose.
